    Most single-page websites are delivered in 2–4 days. Multi-section sites with animations take 5–7 days. AI automation setups usually take 3–5 days depending on complexity.

    Do you work with local businesses?

    Absolutely. I work with restaurants, gaming cafes, gyms, and small businesses everywhere — from local shops to growing brands.

    What's included in a website package?

    Every package includes responsive design, scroll animations, SEO optimization, deployment on Cloudflare Pages, and 30 days of free support.

    Can you redesign my existing website?

    Yes! I specialize in taking outdated websites and rebuilding them with modern, cinematic design. Send me your current site for a free audit.

    Every project comes with 30 days of free maintenance. After that, I offer affordable monthly plans for ongoing updates and technical support.
