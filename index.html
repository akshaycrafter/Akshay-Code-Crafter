07 FAQ

Common questions.

What is your payment structure?

50% advance payment is required before any work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon final delivery. All payments are non-refundable once the project has been initiated — this covers the time, research, and design effort invested from day one.

Who is responsible for hosting and domain?

Hosting and domain are the client's responsibility. I will build and deliver your website files fully ready for deployment. I can guide you through the setup process, but purchasing and managing the hosting plan is on your end.

What happens if I need changes after revisions are complete?

All feedback and design decisions should be shared during the active revision rounds included in your plan. Once those rounds are complete, any additional changes — including design alterations, content updates, or new sections — will be charged separately as a new task.

Do you sign a contract before starting?

Yes. Before any work begins, a simple written agreement is shared outlining the project scope, timeline, payment terms, and deliverables. This protects both parties and ensures there are no surprises at the end.

Who owns the website after delivery?

Once the final payment is made, the delivered website — including all content, design, and assets — belongs to you. The underlying code structure remains proprietary to CodeCrafter and may be used across other projects.
