A 50% advance payment is required before any work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon final delivery and approval. All payments are non-refundable once work has commenced, as this covers the time, research, and design effort invested from day one.

Each project plan includes a set number of revision rounds. Feedback must be provided during the active revision period. Once revisions are complete, any additional changes — including design alterations, content updates, or new features — will be charged separately as a new task.

Delivery & Hosting

A fully built, launch-ready website is delivered as the final product — complete and ready to go live. Hosting and domain registration are the client's responsibility. I will guide you through the setup process if needed.

Scope of Work

Any features, pages, or changes that fall outside the originally agreed scope will require a separate quote and written approval before proceeding. I will not begin work on out-of-scope items without your explicit written confirmation.

Design Approval

The design direction is finalized and approved before development begins. Requests for fundamental redesigns or complete style overhauls after delivery will be treated as a new project requiring a fresh quote.

Source Code

The final deliverable is a fully built, launch-ready website — complete and ready to go live. Raw build files or component-level code are not handed over as part of any package. This ensures quality control and the integrity of the work delivered.
