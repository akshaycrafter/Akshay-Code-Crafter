01

Payment Terms

A 50% advance payment is required before any work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon final delivery and client approval. All payments are non-refundable once work has commenced, as this covers the time, research, and design effort invested from day one.

02

Revision Policy

Revision allowances depend on the selected package. The Standard package includes two revision rounds, the Premium package includes five revision rounds, and AI Automation includes unlimited in-scope refinements during the project. In-scope refinements cover corrections, reasonable updates, and improvements to content or functionality already included in the agreed scope. After launch, the included 30-day support period covers bugs or implementation issues in the delivered work. New pages, features, integrations, major redesigns, or other work outside the agreed scope require a separate quote and written approval before work begins.

03

Delivery & Hosting

The service cards describe the type of work; Standard and Premium identify the delivery and handover tier agreed for the project. The written project scope confirms the applicable tier and specific deliverables.

Standard: The deliverable is the live website link only. Source-code files and deployment handover to a client-controlled hosting environment are not included. Premium: The package includes deployment to the hosting environment agreed for the project, with source-code handover. Hosting and domain registration are the client's responsibility. I will guide you through the setup process if needed, as set out in the written project scope.

04

Scope of Work

Any features, pages, or changes that fall outside the originally agreed scope will require a separate quote and written approval before proceeding. Work on out-of-scope items will not begin without your explicit written confirmation.

05

Intellectual Property

Upon full payment, all delivered website content — including text, images, graphics, and custom design elements — belongs to the client. The underlying code structure, base template, and reusable components remain the intellectual property of CodeCrafter and may be used across other projects.

06

Design Approval

The design direction is finalised and approved before development begins. Requests for fundamental redesigns or complete style overhauls after delivery will be treated as a new project requiring a fresh quote.

07

Source Code

Standard: No source-code or raw build-file handover. The client receives the live website link only. Premium: Source-code handover is included, together with the agreed deployment. Delivery of source files does not automatically transfer CodeCrafter's ownership of its underlying code structure, base templates, or reusable components — see the Intellectual Property section above.

08

Disputes

I prefer to resolve any disputes through open and mutual discussion. CodeCrafter reserves the right to suspend work or withhold deliverables if payment terms are violated or outstanding balances remain unpaid.
